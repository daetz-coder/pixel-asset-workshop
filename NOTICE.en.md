# Sources and distribution

[简体中文](NOTICE.md) · [Project home](README.md)

- Pixel Frog made **Tiny Swords**. The author allows use and modification of the current Free Pack in personal and commercial projects and restricts redistribution, resale and repackaging of those assets, including modified assets. The author's separately listed older release has different terms. [Tiny Swords author page](https://pixelfrog-assets.itch.io/tiny-swords)
- The source pack, user-supplied reference art, cropped source frames and individual frame comparisons are local working materials. The repository ignores `assets/references/tiny-swords/` and `work/`. Obtain the pack from the author to reproduce the Godot scene. [Tiny Swords comparison](examples/tiny-swords/README.md#comparison-with-the-original)
- The training-ground MP4 and screenshot capture the complete running Godot scene. The terrain, scenery and upper-row characters use Pixel Frog's Tiny Swords artwork. The lower row, exported PNG sheets, GIFs and Frontier Outpost use the project's generated artwork. The SpriteFrame screenshots and export record use the same six-unit batch. This notice documents provenance and grants no license to reuse the assets. [First release checklist](docs/release.en.md)
- Wan3.0 Video Prime is an optional service from Alibaba Cloud Model Studio. The local workflow requires no model weights, provider account or service credentials.
